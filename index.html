How to Play

Swap two neighbouring gems to line up 3 or more of the same kind. Drag a gem, or tap one gem then tap its neighbour.

Match 4 in a line to forge a Line Blaster — it clears a whole row or column.

Match 5 in a line to forge a Prism Core — it clears every gem of one colour.

Match in an L or T to forge a Star Bomb — it blasts a 3×3 area.

Swap two special gems together for a much bigger blast. Cascades raise your combo multiplier.

Stone blocks cannot be swapped — clear them by matching gems next to them.

Resonance: some levels have glowing energy veins. Match a crystal on a vein and energy travels along it, shattering every crystal it reaches. Stone blocks absorb the energy and stop it.

Finish the objectives before you run out of moves. Fewer moves used means more stars.
