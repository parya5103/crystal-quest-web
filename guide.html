Crystal Quest: Gem Legends — How to Play and Level Guide

Crystal Quest is a mobile-first match-3 puzzle adventure with 27 handcrafted levels. Swap neighbouring gems, build special clears, manage each level's move limit and explore the story regions as you restore the Crystal Forest.

How to play

  1. Choose a level. Open the level map and select an unlocked stage. Each level shows its objective and move limit before play begins.
  2. Swap adjacent gems. Tap a gem and then one of its neighbours, or drag between neighbouring cells, to make a line of three or more matching gems.
  3. Plan around the objective. Some stages ask you to reach a score, collect certain gem types, clear ice or blockers, or use resonance. Spend moves on the target rather than making matches that do not help.
  4. Use cascades. Cleared gems fall and new gems enter the board. A cascade can create another match automatically, so look for moves that open space and set up follow-on clears.
  5. Finish before moves run out. Complete the objective with as few moves as you can to earn more stars.

Special gems and stronger matches

Matches larger than three can create special gems. They are useful when ordinary matches cannot reach a target or when the board needs a larger clear.

  • Line Blaster: match four gems in a line to create a special gem that clears a full row or column.
  • Prism Core: match five gems in a line to create a special gem that clears every gem of one colour.
  • Star Bomb: make an L- or T-shaped match to create a special gem that blasts a 3×3 area.

Swapping two special gems together triggers a larger combined effect. Before using one, check whether its clear will reach the level objective, remove a blocker or prepare a useful cascade.

Crystal Resonance: levels 21–26

Crystal Resonance is the game's signature board mechanic. In resonance stages, glowing energy veins connect selected cells. Match a crystal on a vein node and energy travels along the path, shattering crystals it reaches. Stone blockers absorb the energy and stop it.

Tip: trace the visible vein before spending a move. A match near the start of a long, unobstructed vein can clear more than a direct match elsewhere. If a stone block interrupts the path, plan a separate match beside it instead of expecting the resonance to pass through.

Guardians and story regions

The adventure moves through three story regions: Crystal Forest, Emberfall Canyon and Starlit Summit. Level 20 introduces the Ember Warden encounter. The campaign finale is The Guardian on level 27, where a shield sequence responds to one gem colour at a time.

During the Guardian encounter, check the colour shown beside the shield counter and match that colour to crack the next segment. Keep enough moves available for the remaining shield stages and the level objective.

Daily Challenge, Relics and saving progress

  • Daily Challenge: a date-seeded puzzle changes each day. Complete it to build your daily streak.
  • Relic Vault: six one-time collectibles are earned at campaign milestones and add pieces to the game's story.
  • Progress: level progress and stars are saved in the current browser. Use Settings → Export save code to back up progress, then Import save code on another device.
  • Offline play: after the PWA has been installed and its files cached, the game can run offline in supported browsers.

Frequently asked questions

How many levels are in Crystal Quest?

The campaign contains 27 handcrafted levels, including the Ember Warden encounter and the level 27 Guardian finale.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No account is required. You can play in a supported browser and optionally install the PWA from the browser's install option.

What should I do when a level feels difficult?

Read the objective first, look for a four- or five-gem match, and check whether a special gem or cascade can clear several target cells at once. In resonance levels, inspect the vein path and any stone blockers before moving.

Can I move my saved game to another device?

Yes. Export a save code from Settings on the first device and import it on the second one.

Game rules and features described here are based on the current Crystal Quest: Gem Legends build. Level objectives can differ, so always check the instructions shown inside the selected level.
