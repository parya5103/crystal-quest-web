Crystal Quest: Gem Legends · GoGo Company

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how Crystal Quest: Gem Legends handles information when you play the game at gogocompany.in/crystalquest, install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA), or choose to enable GoGo push notifications.

Current advertising status: Advertisements are currently disabled, including rewarded video ads. The game does not load an advertising network until an approved provider and a certified consent flow have been configured.

1. Game progress and device storage

The game does not require a player account. Your level progress, stars, settings, story progress, relics and daily challenge state are stored in your browser's local storage on your device under the game's save key (crystal-quest-save-v1). The game also uses a service worker and browser cache to support offline play and app updates. These local records are not automatically synchronized between devices.

The optional save-code feature lets you export progress. The code remains under your control unless you choose to share it. Anyone who obtains a valid save code may be able to import that progress into another copy of the game.

2. Optional push notifications

Push notifications are optional and are not enabled by default. You must explicitly enable notifications in the game's notification settings and grant your browser permission before the game may load Firebase web messaging components and use Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

To register and manage an opted-in device, the game sends GoGo Central the app identifier, Firebase messaging device token, platform, browser user agent and notification preferences. The backend endpoint is https://gogo-central-backend.onrender.com. The user agent can contain browser and device information. Firebase and the backend infrastructure process the information needed to authenticate the anonymous installation, deliver notifications and manage the device registration.

You can turn notifications off in the game's notification settings or revoke notification permission in your browser or device settings. Uninstalling the PWA or clearing site data may remove local preferences; it may not by itself revoke a server-side device registration.

3. Advertising, analytics and third parties

Advertising is currently disabled: no AdSense loader or rewarded-ad provider is active in the shipped game. No advertising cookies or advertising identifiers are intentionally created by the game in this disabled state. If advertising or analytics is introduced later, this policy and the applicable consent experience must be updated before those services are enabled.

When you opt in to notifications, Google Firebase services and GoGo Central are involved as described above. The game also links to the GoGo Company website. Those services and external websites may have their own privacy terms and practices.

4. Information security and retention

Local game data remains in your browser until you clear site data or remove it through browser controls. Notification registration data is handled by GoGo Central and the messaging service to provide the opt-in notification feature. This page does not promise a specific server-side retention period. Contact officialgogocompany@gmail.com to ask about notification registration, retention or deletion requests.

No player-created account, cloud save or payment flow is currently part of the game. Do not include sensitive personal information in a save code or in any message sent through a notification interaction.

5. Children

Crystal Quest is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not intentionally ask players to submit their name, email address or other profile details. Parents or guardians should supervise device-level notification permissions and use of external links.

6. Your choices

  • Play without enabling push notifications.
  • Clear local site data to remove locally stored game progress and settings (export a save code first if you want to keep progress).
  • Revoke notification permission or disable notifications in the game's settings.
  • Contact GoGo Company support or email officialgogocompany@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests relating to notification registration.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when game features, hosting, notification processing or monetization changes. The effective date above will be revised when a material change is published. Check this page periodically.
